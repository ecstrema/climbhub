One of the top bouldering destinations in the world, Fontainebleau is a must-visit for any climber. The forest is home to thousands of boulders, with problems ranging from V0 to V17. The sandstone rock is known for its unique features and technical climbing, making it a favorite among climbers of all levels. With easy access from Paris and a variety of camping and accommodation options, Fontainebleau is the perfect destination for a bouldering trip. Whether you're a beginner looking to improve your skills or an experienced climber seeking a challenge, Fontainebleau has something for everyone.
